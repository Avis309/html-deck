06 · Next quarter

What we learned, and the Q4 plan

What worked

  • Short, honest video beats polished ads.
  • Comparison pages capture people ready to buy.
  • Past customers respond best to email offers.

Q4 plan

  • Double the video budget and publish weekly.
  • Redesign the email sign-up on every page.
  • Test one new paid channel with a small budget.
