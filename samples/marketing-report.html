Landing page test: a shorter form won

Variant A · current

Seven-field form, below the fold

Request a demo
Sign-up rate9.9%

WINNER · +31%

Variant B · new

Two fields, button above the fold

Start free trial
Sign-up rate13.0%
Time to sign up22 s

Ran for 3 weeks on 41,000 visitors, split 50/50. The result is significant (p < 0.01). Variant B rolls out to every page in October.
